Data Governance & Security

Privacy Policy.

InspectaPro is committed to safeguarding your personal information, corporate fleet data, and RTO compliance records with uncompromising bank-grade security protocols.

Last Updated: October 12, 2026

Information We Collect

We collect personal and corporate identifiers necessary to provide seamless vehicle inspection and fleet management services. This includes your full name, professional email address, mobile phone number, billing details, vehicle registration numbers (e.g., TN49BB9012), OBD-II telemetry streams, and regional testing hub location preferences.

How We Use Your Data

Collected data is utilized strictly for scheduling express inspection slots, synchronizing PUCC and fitness certificate records with state RTO registries, dispatching automated WhatsApp/SMS expiry reminders, processing subscription payments, and optimizing our enterprise SaaS telematics engine.

RTO Registry & Government Sharing

To comply with statutory transport regulations, vehicle emission test results and registration identifiers are shared securely via encrypted API handshakes directly with authorized government transport databases. We do not sell or monetize your data to third-party commercial advertisers.

Cloud Vault Security & Encryption

All digital certificates and sensitive audit files stored in our cloud vault are protected using industry-standard 256-bit SSL encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest. Hourly automated snapshots ensure absolute data integrity and high availability.

Cookies & Analytics Tracking

We use essential cookies to maintain user session states, theme toggles (dark/light mode), and language preferences. Anonymous usage metrics are collected to improve web responsiveness and user interface performance across desktop and mobile devices.

Third-Party Enterprise Integrations

When you configure webhooks to connect InspectaPro with Slack, WhatsApp Business API, MS Teams, or AWS cloud services, data transferred to those external endpoints is governed by their respective privacy policies and enterprise data processing agreements.

Your Data Access & Deletion Rights

You retain the full right to access, export, or request permanent deletion of your personal account data and vehicle rosters at any time. Requests can be initiated directly from your client dashboard or by contacting our compliance desk.

Data Retention & Archival Guidelines

Vehicle compliance and inspection certificates are retained in our secure vault for the statutory duration mandated by transport authorities (typically 5 years). Upon account cancellation, non-statutory personal data is purged within 30 business days.

Policy Modifications & Privacy Officer

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological enhancements or regulatory changes. Significant updates will be communicated via email or dashboard alerts. For privacy inquiries, data requests, or compliance concerns, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@inspectapro.io or visit our headquarters in Karur Central Testing Yard, Tamil Nadu.
